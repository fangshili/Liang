# Luminous Signal

## Design Philosophy for Liang App Icons

Light as language. The icon speaks through radiance rather than outline. A single letter, **L**, acts as a beacon—minimal, confident, instantly legible. Around it, color dissolves into atmosphere: warm amber, cool cyan, soft violet. The effect is not decoration but communication, as if the app itself were emitting status.

No black void. Darkness is replaced by deep, saturated hues that hold their own against the Dock and Launchpad. These backgrounds suggest night skies, OLED depths, and terminal warmth without ever collapsing into pure black. Contrast comes from the glow, not from absence.

Diffusion and scatter. Glows are soft and radial; rays break outward in controlled bursts. Nothing is sharp or mechanical. The light behaves like a real source—halation, falloff, subtle interference—giving the icon a tactile, living quality.

Craft and restraint. Each element is placed with care: the L is centered and balanced, the glow never overwhelms the glyph, the color palette is limited to two or three hues. The result should feel like a finished product, not a sketch; something that could sit alongside macOS system icons.
